From browsing to a checked shortlist

How to use a LoveGoBuy spreadsheet

A LoveGoBuy spreadsheet is a product discovery list used to organize finds before ordering through a shopping agent. A useful entry identifies the exact item, source link, variant and reference price. This page provides a HipobuyFind selection; it does not automatically transfer products into a LoveGoBuy account.

1

Find and identify

Search by product type or filter by category. Open the source listing and record the item ID, selected color and size. Similar images can represent different sellers, materials or variants.

2

Compare the evidence

Read the size chart, material description and seller terms. Ask for missing measurements. Treat a seller image as a presentation photo; it does not show the condition of your individual order.

3

Confirm before paying

If you plan to use LoveGoBuy, obtain the original marketplace link from the listing where available and check that the agent accepts it. Confirm the exact variant, current costs and return conditions in your account.

Fit comes first

Measure what matters.

Size names are a starting point. Compare the seller’s measurements with a similar item you already own, measured in the same way. Check whether the chart describes the garment or the wearer’s body.

Before international shipping

Your QC checklist

When warehouse photos are available for your order, inspect the actual item before approving dispatch.

  • Match the color, size label and quantity to the order.
  • Look at stitching, surfaces, seams, fasteners and visible damage.
  • Request a measurement photo if fit is uncertain.
  • Resolve discrepancies within the applicable return window.

QC photos can help identify visible problems. They do not prove authenticity, durability or material composition.

Budget beyond the item price

What will the full order cost?

The CNY amounts in this directory are source reference prices. They are not delivered totals. Build your budget from the charges that apply to your actual order and destination.

Cost componentWhat to confirm
Product and domestic deliveryExact variant price and seller-to-warehouse shipping.
Agent and payment chargesApplicable service fees, optional services and the payment exchange rate.
International freightDestination eligibility, packed dimensions, chargeable weight and the selected shipping line.
Import and delivery chargesApplicable taxes, duties and carrier handling charges for your destination.

A shipping estimate may change after packing. Check the current terms and final quote before authorizing payment.

LoveGoBuy spreadsheet FAQ

Is this the official LoveGoBuy website?

No. This is an independent discovery guide. Its catalog comes from HipobuyFind, and the “LoveGoBuy spreadsheet” and “Buy it now” buttons both open HipobuyFind. This page does not take payments or manage orders.

Are these real products from HipobuyFind?

These six entries were listed on the HipobuyFind homepage when the snapshot was collected. Each entry includes its source item ID and a link to that product page. A listing’s existence does not guarantee stock, quality or authenticity.

Are the prices and stock updated automatically?

No. The directory is a static snapshot dated 19 November 2026. Open the source listing to check current price, variants and availability. The filters search only the six entries on this page.

Can I buy every listed item through LoveGoBuy?

Compatibility has not been verified. Check whether the source page provides an original marketplace link and whether LoveGoBuy can process that exact listing. A product discovery page is not necessarily an importable marketplace URL.

Do the product images show warehouse QC?

No. The images shown here are the listing images supplied through HipobuyFind. Ask your shopping agent for photos of your own order when that service is available.

Do I need Excel or Google Sheets?

No. Browse the cards directly, use the category filter or sort by the displayed CNY reference price. Keep the source link and item ID if you want to build your own comparison sheet.
